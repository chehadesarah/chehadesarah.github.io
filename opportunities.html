Opportunities

Build with us.

CEDAR welcomes researchers who like mathematically serious problems and interdisciplinary work.

General inquiries welcome

Prospective graduate students

If you are considering graduate research in quantum information, mathematical physics, algorithms, scientific computing, or related engineering areas, you are welcome to introduce yourself and describe your interests.

Useful background

  • Linear algebra and mathematical maturity
  • Probability, algebra, graph theory, or numerical methods
  • Interest in quantum information or computation
  • Python or another scientific-computing language is helpful
Project dependent

Undergraduate research

Well-scoped projects can include computational experiments, literature synthesis, graph/invariant calculations, benchmarking, or mathematical exploration. Availability depends on timing and supervision capacity.

A strong inquiry includes

  • Your program/year and relevant coursework
  • Why a specific CEDAR research direction interests you
  • Your coding or mathematical experience
  • The time period you hope to work
Collaborations

Researchers & faculty

We are interested in collaborations that connect mathematical structure with quantum algorithms, control, verification, scientific computing, and engineering systems.

Potential collaborators should feel free to send a concise description of the problem and the connection to current CEDAR work.
